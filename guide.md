# Guide on how to install the assets.

# METHOD 1 | AUTOMATIC 
1. Open a terminal (e.g. Konsole).
2. Copy `curl -fsS https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phybozz/vesktop-monochrome-assets/refs/heads/main/install.sh | bash`
3. Paste (Ctrl+Shift+V) it into your terminal and press Enter.
4. Well done! ;D
# METHOD 2 | MANUAL

## Step 1 | Installing the assets.
Click on these links and download the images: <br>
[tray](assets/tray.png)<br>
[trayUnread](assets/trayUnread.png)\
[splash](assets/splash.webp)

## Step 2 | Adding the assets to Vesktop.
1. Go to the settings in Vesktop and open the Vesktop tab.
2. Click the "Customize App Assets" button.
3. Click each "Customize" button and add the corresponding asset.
4. Well done! ;D
